Kite Fight

Dash through their string three times to cut it!

How to play

  • Let go and your kite spins on the spot.
  • Hold (tap, click or Space) and it dashes where its nose points. Watch the dotted arrow.
  • Dash your kite through their string to notch it. Loop round and do it again: 3 notches and it's cut!
  • Grab power-ups: ✦ glass string (each pass counts double), ➤ speed, ✚ mends a notch.
  • First to cut the other kite twice wins.
